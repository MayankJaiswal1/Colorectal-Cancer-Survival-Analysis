Why this matters

Nearly 3 in 4 patients survive regardless of subgroup. That 74.9% figure becomes the baseline accuracy that any predictive model must beat to be considered useful — a model can hit "high accuracy" simply by guessing the majority class every time, which is exactly what happens in Section 04.

05

Verdict

● flatline confirmed

As provided, this dataset carries no learnable signal for survival — the outcome behaves as if generated independently of every recorded factor.
